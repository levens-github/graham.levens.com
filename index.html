About

I spent 2025–2026 as a photographer aboard Norwegian Cruise Line’s Pride of America, sailing the Hawaiian Islands every week. The job was portraits, events, and sales at the same time: set up the lights, pose a family in ninety seconds, make the shy person comfortable, then sell the prints that night in the gallery. I finished the year as the Photography Department’s top salesperson and with more positive guest comment cards than anyone on the team.

Before the ship I worked front desk and camp staff at a mountain retreat, lifeguarded, taught ropes and belay, and put in kitchen and counter shifts. I am an Eagle Scout. I like long days, busy rooms, and people who did not think they wanted their picture taken.

Based in Rancho Cucamonga, California. Open to portrait, event, resort, and attraction photography.
