Graham Levens

Photographer · Southern California

Graham Levens spent the past year at sea with Norwegian Cruise Line, photographing guests aboard Pride of America on her weekly circuit of the Hawaiian Islands. He ran studio portrait sessions, formal nights, and port-day shoots, edited and delivered the work the same day, and finished the contract leading the ship’s Photography Department in sales.

The years before were spent at Alpine Retreat & Camp, a Christ-centered camp near Lake Arrowhead that promises guests “a family of hospitality experts,” greeting arriving groups, running the challenge course, and in the lifeguard chair, where he once helped save a child’s life. That work taught him what his guests now write about most: he makes people comfortable quickly. He is an Eagle Scout and holds STCW maritime safety and Red Cross lifeguard certifications.
